# Rulings by the rules owner (they override or complete the rulebook text)
R1 Minor face value = 100 (rulebook states none).
R2 Phase-2 minor start price = highest start value <= half the winning bid, minimum 50.
R3 Minor bid flow: player pays the full bid to the bank. Phase 1: treasury 100, start price 50. Phase 2: treasury 2 x start price. Phase 3+: treasury = full bid. The bank covers any difference.
R4 LNWR conversion: bank pays the whole start price into the treasury, player pays nothing and receives the 1-unit director certificate, no extra concession discount.
R5 Director succession (4.4.2): the director may sell everything only if another player holds at least a director's certificate's worth of units (2; LNWR 1) and more than the director's remaining holding; ties -> player nearest after the seller in player order.
R6 Rule 10.1.1 "nothing sold" in the first stock round: bidding does NOT count as a sale by itself; buying shares, selling shares and converting a concession count. (Open conflict: in phase 1 only bids are possible. Owner to settle; provisional: a bid that wins an item at resolution counts.)
R7 Train export: the 4.11.6 exports (one per unsold minor) count as L trains and never trigger a phase. The 4.11.7 export (unsold box-1 minor) takes the current buyable pool, runs the phase check, and when L/2 is exhausted exports a 3-train.
R8 If the winner of an item cannot pay at resolution, the item goes to the next highest bidder who can; if nobody can, it stays in its box and counts as having had a bid.
R9 From phase 5: held concessions disappear, concession boxes are cleared and never refilled.
R10 4.4.7: a player over the certificate limit must sell (forced) down to the limit at the first opportunity; while over the limit and able to sell, the player may not buy or bid.
R11 Deferred (out of scope): P16 exceptions, P9 exception, 4.8 direct formation from phase 5, stock-market arrows (provisional: off the right end -> up one row same column; off the left end -> down one row).
R12 Market data: left-aligned rows as printed; a price drop = move down one row in the same column; no move if the cell does not exist.
R13 100% sold test for the 4.12 price rise: treasury and bank pool both hold zero units.
R14 Loan interest at stock-round end: ceil(loans / 2) added to loans.
R15 Selling shares and buying shares both set the flag "a sale happened this stock round".
